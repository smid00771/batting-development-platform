From availability to published teams

A clearer week for club volunteers.

01 / KNOW

Availability is confirmed.

Players mark the dates they can play, including each day of a two-day match. Silence doesn’t count as available.

02 / SELECT

Build the team in the meeting.

Import your playing list, add player roles and select by name. Keep track of available players still waiting for a place.

03 / BRACKET

Match the two weeks.

Find players whose availability fits Day 1 and Day 2. Compare similar roles and other options before naming your brackets.

04 / PUBLISH

Captains contribute. Selectors decide.

Share drafts privately for captains’ comments. Make adjustments, then the Head of Selections publishes the teams for players.
