A few useful answers.

Can we start before our fixtures are released?

Yes. Set up your Selection Groups and teams first, then add or import fixtures when they become available.

Do we have to use Club Batting?

No. Mirelion Select is a standalone product. Batting subscriptions and Player Plans are not required to select teams.

Can we choose when the trial starts?

Yes. Arrange your 21-day free trial for a date that suits your club. No card is required and nothing is automatically charged.
