How it works

01

Confirm availability

Players mark when they can play, including each day of a two-day match. Unconfirmed players are not treated as available.

02

Select teams

Import your playing list, add player roles and choose teams. See who is available but not yet selected.

03

Pair players across two days

Match Day 1 and Day 2 availability for players sharing a place. Compare their batting and bowling roles.

04

Check and publish

Captains review draft teams. The Head of Selections makes any changes and publishes to players.

Use on its own or with Club Batting

Select teams from your playing list without a batting subscription. Add Club Batting later using the same club and player accounts.
