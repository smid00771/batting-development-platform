A few useful answers.

Can we use Club Batting on its own?

Yes. Club Batting works independently. You can keep your existing selection process and add Mirelion Select later if it suits your club.

What happens if we add Mirelion Select later?

Your club, people and batting work stay in place. A Club Admin can add product access and choose which Selection Groups participate in Club Batting.

How long is the trial?

Choose your start date for a full 60-day Club Batting trial. No card is required and nothing is automatically charged.
