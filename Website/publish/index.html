A few useful answers.

Can Mirelion Select work without Club Batting?

Yes. Your club can manage its playing list, availability and selections without a Club Batting subscription or Player Plans.

Do our players need accounts before we can select teams?

No. Selectors can add and select players straight away. A player needs a linked account to manage their own availability and read team messages in the app.

We already use Club Batting. Do we need another account?

No. Use your existing sign-in. Both products belong to the same club account, and a Club Admin can manage the club’s product access.

How do the free trials work?

Choose a product and your start date: 21 days for Mirelion Select, 60 days for Club Batting. You can choose both. No card is required and nothing is automatically charged.
