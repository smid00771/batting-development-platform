# Previous-example matching and reliable result wording — 0.8.62.289

Preparing a Results, Century or Five wickets background from a previous example now also requests its supported lettering, colours and layout. The existing Prepare background / Start from previous example action performs the style request before opening artwork review. Use background saves that completed suggestion with the prepared image; it does not initiate another AI request. The assembled graphic is reviewed before confirmation, and Undo restores the preceding layout. Editing an existing background does not repeat initial matching. No new button or mandatory manual wording control is added.

Results wording is one paired presentation setting for the whole graphic: WON/LOST, WIN/LOSS or WIN/DEF. Imported outcomes still decide which word appears. Existing saved designs retain WIN/DEF until a change is requested. Font or wording changes remove old cropped lettering. Crops are used only as a complete, source-bound pair containing the selected words; incomplete pairs use one generated font for both outcomes. Draw, tie, no result, unfinished and unresolved matches retain their existing factual treatment.

Initial example matching may adjust geometry. Subsequent Match our example and wording-only requests explicitly preserve the user's slider positions. AI cannot change match facts, source grades or asset paths. Initial matching also cannot copy an example's week label or grade labels, extract badges or launch another artwork edit. The renderer keeps its existing shared-versus-per-row opponent-logo logic.

Save retries reuse the style suggestion, and account/view/week/layout/revision guards discard late work. Club permissions, caller-JWT storage access, existing AI allowance/rate limits, optimistic saves, immutable confirmed graphics and final posting review remain intact. The client forwards award scope and unfinished-match inclusion. Older clients receive their prior supported schema. No database migration or credentials change is required.
