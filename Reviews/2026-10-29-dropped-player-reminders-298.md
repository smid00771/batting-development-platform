# Dropped-player reminders and disabled prediction navigation — 0.8.62.298

Selection Board → More → Show dropped players now checks the displayed teams once and saves the grade-drop reminders. Refreshing, saving a team, changing screens and reopening the app reuse those saved reminders. Normal context reads do not run the historical grade comparison. Returning-player availability and the two existing on-demand analysis actions retain their current behaviour.

Mark as spoken to saves the shared completion and removes that player's highlight and control. Other outstanding reminders remain. Completion survives another explicit check; other authorised staff receive it on their next normal refresh, without a new polling loop. The request applies to selected players generally, regardless of batting or bowling role. Available, not selected remains a separate workflow.

The existing club membership, Selection Group view/edit permissions, previous-captain and coach access, mutation receipts and revision checks are preserved. Marking a reminder works on locked teams. Ordinary players and unrelated captains cannot read private reminders. Explicitly unavailable, inactive or no-longer-selected players and changed fixture identities do not retain obsolete visible markers. The new table and helpers are private, with RLS enabled and direct client privileges revoked.

Database migration `dropped_player_reminders` was applied to the existing project on 29 October 2026. Synthetic transaction tests passed after deployment, including idempotent capture and marking, locked teams, shared completion, stale revisions, availability, membership and role restrictions. The test temporarily replaces the historical comparison functions with exceptions to prove ordinary refreshes and marking a saved reminder do not call them, then rolls everything back. No real player was marked or real team changed during verification.

The delivered demo uses the shared application code and matching fictional snapshot behaviour. Four complete-demo tests cover explicit calculation, refresh/save persistence, completion, invalidation and safe retry. Selection Group, transfer, quiet returning-player, analysis, captain, role and navigation regressions passed. The picker test now drains the shell’s already-scheduled startup upkeep before measuring interactions, avoiding a timing-sensitive false failure without weakening its no-request assertion. Two older function-extraction UI suites also fail on the unchanged 0.8.62.297 base because their harness omits current dependencies; they are not presented as passing. JavaScript syntax, generated-demo freshness and whitespace checks passed.
