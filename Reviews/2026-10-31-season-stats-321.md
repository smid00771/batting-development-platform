# Season stats and quieter coaching history — .321

Season stats now puts batting average first and orders the list from highest average to lowest. Aligned columns show average, strike rate, runs, innings and not outs. Average and strike rate use two decimal places. Undefined averages remain unavailable, including unbeaten players, and missing balls or incomplete figures never become invented strike rates. Each record stays within its existing grade, competition and format.

Grade, format and player search share one toolbar on desktop and adapt to phone width. Search remains available for small or empty result sets. Match history, secondary totals and the complete form guide are available by expanding a player; the repeated form labels no longer dominate the list. Batting grade comparisons also include strike rate when known. Bowling keeps its wicket order and exposes average, economy, overs and innings together.

Club Batting-only staff retain the direct Menu → Season stats entry. Select and linked clubs retain Club Selections → More → Season stats. Both use the same on-demand view and existing access checks.

In coaching conversations, recognised automatic observation creation/edit entries are retained under a closed Change history disclosure. They no longer replace an actual comment as the latest update. The latest comment appears once, earlier comments remain in history, and repeated scorecard/innings details stay collapsed even after opening Details and history. Editing, review, completion, independent reflection and saved records are preserved.
