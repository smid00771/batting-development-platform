# Captain Selection Board — 0.8.62.202

Captains can read current drafts throughout selection and after publication. Captain-only access cannot change a lineup, fixture, lock or publication. Existing selector and administrator permissions remain additive.

The Selection Board replaces the Captain Checks and Player conversations pages. Team comments appear beneath the relevant team and retain their assigned-captain/selector privacy. Player follow-up is a compact section on the board. Coaches retain their follow-up access without gaining draft-team access. Old page links lead to the board. The separate Send to captains action is retired; ordinary players still see published teams only.

Follow-up includes moves down a grade and active players omitted after the previous completed round in the same competition, season and format. It uses published lineups, including PlayCricket corrections where imported. Without a scorecard, the published selection is the available evidence of who played. Recorded no-play fixtures are excluded from omission evidence; players continuing a two-day match are not flagged. Available, explicitly unavailable and unconfirmed availability remain distinct.

The previous-round captain, selectors and coaches share spoken-to status, attribution and time. Re-selection removes an omission from the list. If a fixture is cancelled or another fixture becomes the place for that follow-up, the same saved marker is retained. Revision checks and request IDs protect against stale updates and duplicate saves. Comments refer to the team revision viewed and retain text after a failed save.
