# Selection conversations and format views — 0.8.62.259

Selection comparisons were hidden behind individual form icons, and one numbered link per person hid other qualifying options. The board and Compare grades now give every qualifying conversation a matching number at both ends, outline the associated players, and draw a connection through the column gap when both grades sit side by side. Stacked cards retain matching numbers. Focus or hover emphasises the related players; opening the form guide shows the first conversation's evidence. Lines respond to resizing and are removed when indicators are hidden or the view changes.

Close batting or bowling calls join the existing stronger cases and explicit trade-offs. They retain the same competition, adjacent-grade conversion, format, role, keeper, workload and sample checks. The rough comparison margin is 15%; an alert is a selection conversation, not a prediction or an instruction to change the team. Names, reputation and pathway labels do not determine eligibility. Actual figures and rough equivalents remain distinguishable. The guide explicitly does not rate fielding; discussion should use recorded observations alongside it.

Limited-overs bowling flags now allow strong wicket-taking with economy up to 135% of the pooled grade reference; the expensive-bowling check starts at 150%. Long-form and T20 form thresholds stay unchanged. A limited-overs economy-led conversation may show a strong candidate with at least 15% better converted economy and wicket-taking within 15% of the incumbent, labelled as a trade-off. Historical exports still pool roles and formats: these are explicit trial allowances, not newly measured format-specific baselines.

Season stats has an explicit format selector in both totals and grade comparisons. Line-ups and figures stay within that format; long-form is separately accessible and remains selected even when it has no figures. Unknown formats remain separate, and aliases use explicit source labels, never inferred dates. Last published line-ups remain the default within the selected format, with dates and current drafts still available. Demo browser Back preserves the comparison, selected format and bowling/batting view.
