A few practical things

Before you jump in.

Can I explore without an account?

Yes. The demo uses fictional people and includes preparation and published examples. Use Explore to try Workshop comparisons, group assignment, Player Plans and Coach Conversations. Example edits last until reset or reload. Real sign-up, invitations, email and AI chat require your live club.

Does every club use the same batting philosophy?

Your club defines its approach. Invited contributors compare and discuss How We Bat options. The Philosophy Lead chooses and confirms the approach, then players develop individual plans using prompts based on it. The Philosophy Lead can edit those prompts before confirming them.

Who gets the club started?

A Club Admin looks after registration, roles and access. People can join during preparation as players or non-playing staff. The Philosophy Lead brings the club’s approach together with the contributors; players build plans once it is published. Lead Admin responsibility can be formally handed over later.

How does a review help the next training session?

A completed My Player Plan is arranged in expandable Core, T20, Limited Overs and Long Form tiles. Players choose up to two current focuses from saved plan answers, coaching conversations or their own innings reflections. Those priorities stay visible on Player Home, How We Train and innings review. Choose Keep working on this, Adjust the focus or Discuss it after a review. Coach proposals need player acceptance; changing a focus does not rewrite the Player Plan.

Can coaches prepare for a whole Playing Group?

Training preparation brings together current focuses, conversations needing attention and review dates for the players they already have permission to see. Choose a Playing Group and look for shared themes when planning practice. Each player keeps their own focus, and staff access stays within the club’s existing permissions.

Can players prepare and keep a season record?

Yes. Prepare in My Innings early enough to guide the week’s practice. Enter the match details, then start with Match-ups, open directly underneath. Add a bowler and choose scoring options. Playing the same team again? Copy the bowlers into this match preparation. sits below Match-ups. Choose a previous entry, then Copy into this preparation to bring across its bowlers, scoring shots, shot explanations, fielders to watch and personal contest. Review the copied details for this match and select any training focuses again. Fielders to watch and Personal contest remain optional. There is no separate match-focus entry. Existing current focuses remain on Player Home, How We Train and review screens, and older saved match-focus notes remain in View. Tick Make this a training focus for this week, then save preparation: that match-up appears in How We Train and is shared with permitted coaches. It leaves active training views after the match date or once played, cancelled or marked Did not bat. The original record remains.

Choose individual scoring shots from the saved Player Plan; colour and a tick show selection. Under Add another scoring shot, type a name to reveal Add shot to preparation. That button adds the shot to the draft and then disappears. The selected shot and any warning panel come into view near the top without opening the phone keyboard. Every selected added shot still outside the saved plan needs an answer to Why are you choosing this shot? before saving upcoming preparation, even when training focus is selected. Deselecting the shot or successfully using Add to My Player Plan removes that requirement. The latter immediately saves a trusted shot to Core; it does not prove practice.

Save preparation saves the bowler details, shots, reasons and training choices together. The form does not auto-save. Any outside-plan shot raises a Coach Conversation; no training tick gets a stronger caution and multiple outside-plan shots get the strongest. Coaches see the player’s reason. An unticked box does not prove no practice. Recording an innings, Did not bat or Match cancelled does not force new reasons into earlier preparation.

One conversation covers each preparation. Changed shots, reasons or training choices update and alert the same thread; changed outside-plan intentions can reopen it after completion. Unchanged saves do not duplicate or reopen it. Removing all outside-plan shots does not close it automatically: use Discussed / Actioned when dealt with. Only flagged bowler and shot names, reasons, training choices, date, format and opposition are shared through that conversation. Selected training match-ups also share their training cues. Fielders, personal contests, match-up reviews and other preparation stay private.

Each entry stays in one compact row. Choose View for its details. You can add an innings without preparing first, and no phone use at training is required. Explore My Innings in the demo.

Can a captain quickly review the whole team?

Match review sits beside Players for authorised staff. Choose Playing Group / grade on the first line with the match details. The permitted roster loads automatically; entering match details or choosing a grade does not save a review. Search for a player, then mark Yes, Mostly, Not really, Has no plan or DNB. Add a dismissal assessment where useful and choose Save match review once. Optionally add an Australian PlayCricket scorecard link, choose your team and check the player matches. Selected scores appear in the final column. Saving confirms those scores and updates official prediction results where an entry exists. Result-entry permission is required, including when confirming your own score; Admin can correct results. Loading the link alone saves no innings or result. A closed batting innings can be imported while a two-day match continues; unfinished innings are preview only. Keep the original match-start date even when players bat the following weekend. Set Result date to when the score occurred. Batting and prediction awards use that date, while match identity and voting deadlines stay fixed. Already released awards need an explicit update. All changed rows save together, including rows hidden by the search.

The Innings selector switches between the first and second innings within a match; save draft changes before switching. Saved history groups the same author’s fixture and grade with an innings selector. Saved match reviews is a separate section within Match review, with grade, date, format and player filters. Choose Edit review to update the same review and save changes to its existing entries. New match review / Clear entry resets the form without deleting saved records; unsaved changes require confirmation. Club Admin can preview deleting a saved review. The preview distinguishes removable review entries from protected shared data: other observers’ contributions, player data and official scores remain.

Match review, individual Match Observations and My Innings share one innings per player, match date, format, opposition and innings number. Different observers keep their own attributed contributions. Compact rows open with View; format filters include All formats. Comments, YouTube links and follow-ups can be added later and remain when quick answers are edited. Players give their independent reflection before plan-related staff assessments or video are revealed. Has no plan points to completing the Player Plan; DNB does not ask for a batting reflection. Existing staff access still applies.

What is Make your prediction?

An optional club game around the cricket. Make your own score prediction in Prepare for a match and tick the option to enter the club game. Members predict teammates’ scores from the compact weekly table. Predictions lock automatically at midnight as the match date begins in the club timezone.

Player predictions, club estimates and named peer predictions stay hidden until authorised staff release the completed period’s results. Admin or permitted staff confirm actual scores, which also appear in My Innings. Awards use the recorded result date, including for two-day games. Your private match preparation and coaching records are not shared.

In Edit club setup, Club Admin ticks the awards the club wants. Score above the club’s prediction for batting points, with an allowance for shorter formats. Best Judge rewards getting closest to teammates’ actual scores. Other optional awards recognise backing a teammate, an underestimate proved wrong, or the season’s regular doubter. Wildest prediction is no longer offered. Award descriptions use everyday language; enabled season awards include their tally.

Selected awards appear under Weekly Awards, Four-week awards or Season awards. Clubs can set prizes and collection conditions; these details are visible only to authorised staff. Four-week periods run from a chosen first award week. Live or held season standings are a club choice. Results history keeps released announcements; later corrections need an explicit updated announcement. Staff can still reveal completed predictions and scores if no awards are selected.

Club Conversation is off until Club Admin selects its checkbox. When enabled, members can share named weekly comments, remove their own messages or report a concern. Turning it off hides the panel and stops new posts without deleting earlier messages.

What earns the engagement award?

Your club can include or turn off this award in Edit club setup. Training Check-in remains available either way.

This is for players’ own cricket activity. Playing coaches, captains and admins qualify on the same terms as every other player. Managing roles or groups, Match review, entering results and coaching other players earn no credit. Non-playing staff cannot earn it.

A qualifying club week needs any two of Prepare, Training check-in, and Review and continue. A weekly award needs one qualifying week. A four-week award needs at least two different qualifying weeks in the four-week period; one activity in each of two weeks is insufficient. Four-week engagement awards need two qualifying club weeks within the displayed fixed period. Each qualifying week needs two different activity categories. A later period does not reuse those weeks. The same short activities feed both cadences; there is no extra four-week form. Preparation needs useful bowling and scoring choices for a match within the next 14 days, saved before its date begins. A private training check-in connects to an existing focus or saved plan answer: say what happened and choose a next step. It records an intention; use the existing focus or Coach Conversations controls to make a change or request. A review needs an independent innings reflection and a deliberately saved next step within 14 days of that innings.

Each category counts once in the week its qualifying record is saved. Repeated clicks, page visits, votes and comments earn nothing. The training check-in is self-reported; Couldn’t this week can count as reconsidering the next step, never as proof of practice. Injury, non-selection or a cancelled match needs no invented innings; useful preparation and a check-in can qualify. There are no daily streaks or catch-up penalties.

Engagement does not require entering the score game. Everyone’s qualifying personal player activity counts automatically. Only the award, name and award-history counts appear in released club honours; private answers and coaching notes keep their existing access. Each club names the reward, chooses weekly or four-week and may set How to claim the engagement prize or leave it blank. Once staff release the completed period, engagement awards appear with the other weekly or four-week honours, with the correct prize and claim condition. A player’s own activity progress remains private and available before release; confirmation of the engagement award waits for the announcement. For example, a club might say Must be at the pub on Friday to claim. Activities determine qualification, staff release the award announcement, and the club handles actual prize collection; the app does not check attendance. There is no attendance or redemption workflow. This recognises recorded engagement, not improved batting, and no phone use at training is required.

Can players ask for help or share a video?

Yes. Players can request a Coach Conversation and choose a preferred club member, or leave it to the coaching team. Coaches can attach a YouTube link to a Match or Training Observation; players can include one in their request or My Innings entry. Video files are uploaded to YouTube. Club Batting stores only the link and optional note; discussion and next steps stay in the app. To show one ball, share a separately trimmed video, or use a start-time link with a note identifying the few seconds to watch. A start-time link does not hide the rest of the video. An unlisted video can be viewed and forwarded by anyone with its link.
