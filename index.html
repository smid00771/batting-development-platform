A few practical things

Before you jump in.

Can I explore without an account?

Yes. The demo uses fictional people and includes preparation and published examples. Use Explore to try Workshop comparisons, group assignment, Player Plans and Coach Conversations. Example edits last until reset or reload. Real sign-up, invitations, email and AI chat require your live club.

Does every club use the same batting philosophy?

Your club defines its approach. Invited contributors compare and discuss How We Bat options. The Philosophy Lead chooses and confirms the approach, then players develop individual plans using prompts based on it. The Philosophy Lead can edit those prompts before confirming them.

Who gets the club started?

A Club Admin looks after registration, roles and access. People can join during preparation as players or non-playing staff. The Philosophy Lead brings the club’s approach together with the contributors; players build plans once it is published. Lead Admin responsibility can be formally handed over later.

How does a review help the next training session?

Players choose up to two current focuses from saved plan answers, coaching conversations or their own innings reflections. Those priorities stay visible on Player Home, How We Train and innings review. Choose Keep working on this, Adjust the focus or Discuss it after a review. Coach proposals need player acceptance; changing a focus does not rewrite the Player Plan.

Can coaches prepare for a whole Playing Group?

Training preparation brings together current focuses, conversations needing attention and review dates for the players they already have permission to see. Choose a Playing Group and look for shared themes when planning practice. Each player keeps their own focus, and staff access stays within the club’s existing permissions.

Can players prepare and keep a season record?

Yes. Prepare in My Innings early enough to guide the week’s practice. Enter the match details, then start with Match-ups, open directly underneath. Add a bowler and choose scoring options. Playing the same team again? Copy the bowlers into this match preparation. sits below Match-ups. Choose a previous entry, then Copy into this preparation to bring across its bowlers, scoring shots, shot explanations, fielders to watch and personal contest. Review the copied details for this match and select any training focuses again. Fielders to watch and Personal contest remain optional. There is no separate match-focus entry. Existing current focuses remain on Player Home, How We Train and review screens, and older saved match-focus notes remain in View. Tick Make this a training focus for this week, then save preparation: that match-up appears in How We Train and is shared with permitted coaches. It leaves active training views after the match date or once played, cancelled or marked Did not bat. The original record remains.

Choose individual scoring shots from the saved Player Plan; colour and a tick show selection. Under Add another scoring shot, type a name to reveal Add shot to preparation. That button adds the shot to the draft and then disappears. The selected shot and any warning panel come into view near the top without opening the phone keyboard. Every selected added shot still outside the saved plan needs an answer to Why are you choosing this shot? before saving upcoming preparation, even when training focus is selected. Deselecting the shot or successfully using Add to My Player Plan removes that requirement. The latter immediately saves a trusted shot to Core; it does not prove practice.

Save preparation saves the bowler details, shots, reasons and training choices together. The form does not auto-save. Any outside-plan shot raises a Coach Conversation; no training tick gets a stronger caution and multiple outside-plan shots get the strongest. Coaches see the player’s reason. An unticked box does not prove no practice. Recording an innings, Did not bat or Match cancelled does not force new reasons into earlier preparation.

One conversation covers each preparation. Changed shots, reasons or training choices update and alert the same thread; changed outside-plan intentions can reopen it after completion. Unchanged saves do not duplicate or reopen it. Removing all outside-plan shots does not close it automatically: use Discussed / Actioned when dealt with. Only flagged bowler and shot names, reasons, training choices, date, format and opposition are shared through that conversation. Selected training match-ups also share their training cues. Fielders, personal contests, match-up reviews and other preparation stay private.

Each entry stays in one compact row. Choose View for its details. You can add an innings without preparing first, and no phone use at training is required. Explore My Innings in the demo.

Can a captain quickly review the whole team?

Team review sits beside Players for authorised staff. Choose Playing Group / grade on the first line with the match details. The permitted roster loads automatically; entering match details or choosing a grade does not save a review. Search for a player, then mark Yes, Mostly, Not really, Has no plan or DNB. Add a dismissal assessment where useful and choose Save team review once. All changed rows save together, including rows hidden by the search.

Saved team reviews is a separate section within Team review, with grade, date, format and player filters. Choose Edit team to update the same review and save changes to its existing entries. New team review / Clear entry resets the form without deleting saved records; unsaved changes require confirmation. Club Admin can preview deleting a saved review. The preview distinguishes removable review entries from protected shared data: other observers’ contributions, player data and official scores remain.

Team review, individual Match Observations and My Innings share one innings per player, match date, format, opposition and innings number. Different observers keep their own attributed contributions. Compact rows open with View; format filters include All formats. Comments, YouTube links and follow-ups can be added later and remain when quick answers are edited. Players give their independent reflection before plan-related staff assessments or video are revealed. Has no plan points to completing the Player Plan; DNB does not ask for a batting reflection. Existing staff access still applies.

What is Make your call?

An optional club game around the cricket. In match preparation, a player can keep a predicted score private or explicitly enter Make your call. Club members, including non-playing staff, make one named run estimate on another player’s entry and can join the weekly discussion. Make your call sits at the right end of the main menu, after My Innings for players. The board starts with compact player rows: find a player or opposition, optionally show only Needs my prediction, then choose Enter your prediction on a row. Only your own peer prediction is visible to you before release. The player’s score call, club estimate, other votes and entry results stay hidden, even after you vote. Change prediction reopens your saved vote. After the weekly or four-week announcement, View shows that entry’s released details. Published player predictions freeze on entry; peer votes close at midnight at the start of the nominated match date in the club timezone. There is no fixture import or manual baseline to maintain.

At least three peer estimates are needed; their median, rounded up, becomes the club estimate. This reduces the effect of one extreme call; it does not prevent majority coordination. Only an authorised staff result scores. Nobody can record their own result, including Admin; another authorised scorer is needed. That result flows into My Innings without asking the player to enter it again. Points are the runs above that estimate, with zero as the minimum. The weekly or four-week winner has the largest single-innings margin in that period; season awards track total points and, separately, how many times a player beat the estimate. You believed in them recognises the largest call above the other voters’ median that the player reached. Wildest call is the largest miss in either direction: playful recognition, not evidence of cheating or an automatic debt. That’s just nasty flags a named vote at least 15 runs and 50% below the rounded-up median of at least three other votes. Its small amber bar appears inside the expanded named votes after release. The award for the selected period goes to the low predictor only when the official played score reaches that other-vote median; the biggest qualifying gap wins, with ties sharing. This adds no minus points or automatic debt. If every prediction is exact, there is no Wildest call award. The older label “forfeit” meant an optional friendly club challenge; the app enforces no payment or punishment. Ties share honours; entries without enough votes do not earn club-estimate awards. Clubs can also opt into a season Grinch award, choose its name and what it entails. It is off by default and recognises repeated low predictions proved wrong, with at least three across two weeks and at least half the voter’s comparable calls.

Best Judge recognises accurate predictions of other players’ official scores alongside the batting awards. Lowest average absolute error wins: ten runs too high and ten too low count equally. Weekly eligibility needs three settled peer predictions in that club week; four-week eligibility needs six across two club weeks. The season tally needs ten across three club weeks. Four-week and season averages pool every eligible prediction, not weekly averages. The board shows the average to two decimal places, prediction and week counts, and progress to qualify. The season Best Judge and season batting tallies start collapsed: choose View tally to expand either, then Hide tally to close it. Unrounded averages determine ties, with exact ties sharing honours. Open entries, pending results, Did not bat, cancellations and the player’s own prediction do not count. Genuine ducks and not-outs count. Corrections update the private award preview and live season standings; released snapshots change only when staff publish an update. Best Judge does not require three votes on each entry. Clubs choose separate recurring and season prizes, or leave them blank for recognition only. The average covers only the calls made; different chosen innings and opportunities mean this is not a controlled comparison of skill.

Club Admin chooses Edit club setup to enable the game, set the timezone, season start and week-start day, and name each reward and choose the First award week. Recurring awards can be Weekly or Every 4 weeks. This week’s awards groups weekly prizes, including engagement. Weekly recognition groups those with no prize entered. Four-week awards appears only when some are configured; season tallies remain separate. Each prize can have its own optional collection condition, such as Must be at the pub on Friday to collect, shown with that prize. This applies to weekly, four-week and season awards. It does not change who earns the award; the club handles collection. Leave the condition blank when none applies. Each award has a How it works explanation with its rule, a short example and eligibility. Tap or click it, or use Enter or Space from the keyboard; desktop hover also previews it. Setup can be reopened after saving. Tick all permitted result scorers and choose Save permissions once; the grants do not widen player access. These are club arrangements, with no payment collection.

Choose any date in Show a date in the week to see its club week, even with no entries. A date selector appears with four-week awards and identifies the full block containing that date. New discussions may start this week or within the next four weeks; existing rounds remain available. Four-week awards run in fixed 28-day blocks from the club’s First award week: weeks 1–4, then 5–8. These are neither calendar months nor a rolling window. Batting and prediction awards use the match date within that block; engagement groups the weeks in which players saved their own activities. Prize and cadence edits apply to current and future periods; closed-period settings are retained, and the same records cannot earn a second award under a new frequency. The First award week can be corrected before it is used, then stays fixed so later edits cannot move entries between prizes. Keep the same week-start day while four-week awards are active. Frequency changes wait for a safe completed-period boundary, so the same activity or innings cannot win again under a new schedule. With weekly-only awards, a change to the week-start day displays its effective date and preserves earlier records; the last open week can extend to reach the new day, avoiding an extra short award week. Named entries and game results do not expose private plans, preparation or coaching records, and the game does not rate batting ability or plan adherence.

Award results wait for the club’s announcement. Players can see prize names, collection conditions and How it works throughout the week. Club Admin, Head Coach, Coach or Captain privately previews the chosen weekly or four-week awards and explicitly releases them when ready. That announcement also reveals the included players’ calls, named votes, club estimates and entry results. Review & release awards initially selects the most recent completed week or four-week period in the club timezone, independently of the main board. Choose an older completed period when needed. The preview, confirmation button and success message show its exact dates, with View announced awards afterwards. Current and future periods cannot be released even if voting has already closed. Release also waits if an included call somehow still has open voting; deadlines are never shortened. For a Monday-start club, Friday 2 October 2026 defaults to the completed 21–27 September week, not the current 28 September–4 October week. An entry added after an announcement remains blind until a later release includes it. Staff enter official scores in a separate permission-controlled editor; their ordinary prediction board stays blind too. Season standings are live by default so members can follow provisional leaders. Club Admin may instead choose Hold until released; in that mode staff release season standings separately. Weekly and four-week awards always wait for release. Live season totals use settled results; neither those totals nor a separate season release reveal individual calls. Entering a score or opening the preview does not announce winners. Released honours and call details retain the reviewed results until staff preview and publish an update, so later scores and corrections do not quietly rewrite the announcement. This releases results inside Club Batting; it does not send emails or post to social media.

What earns the engagement award?

This is for players’ own cricket activity. Playing coaches, captains and admins qualify on the same terms as every other player. Managing roles or groups, Team review, entering results and coaching other players earn no credit. Non-playing staff cannot earn it.

A qualifying club week needs any two of Prepare, Training check-in, and Review and continue. A weekly award needs one qualifying week. A four-week award needs at least two different qualifying weeks in the four-week period; one activity in each of two weeks is insufficient. Four-week engagement awards need two qualifying club weeks within the displayed fixed period. Each qualifying week needs two different activity categories. A later period does not reuse those weeks. The same short activities feed both cadences; there is no extra four-week form. Preparation needs useful bowling and scoring choices for a match within the next 14 days, saved before its date begins. A private training check-in connects to an existing focus or saved plan answer: say what happened and choose a next step. It records an intention; use the existing focus or Coach Conversations controls to make a change or request. A review needs an independent innings reflection and a deliberately saved next step within 14 days of that innings.

Each category counts once in the week its qualifying record is saved. Repeated clicks, page visits, votes and comments earn nothing. The training check-in is self-reported; Couldn’t this week can count as reconsidering the next step, never as proof of practice. Injury, non-selection or a cancelled match needs no invented innings; useful preparation and a check-in can qualify. There are no daily streaks or catch-up penalties.

Engagement does not require entering the score game. Players separately choose Share my engagement awards with the club; only the award, name and award-history counts are shown publicly within the club. Each club names the reward, chooses weekly or four-week and may set How to claim the engagement prize or leave it blank. Once staff release the completed period, shared awards appear with the other weekly or four-week honours, with the correct prize and claim condition. A player’s own activity progress remains private and available before release; confirmation of the engagement award waits for the announcement. For example, a club might say Must be at the pub on Friday to claim. Activities determine qualification, staff release the award announcement, and the club handles actual prize collection; the app does not check attendance. There is no attendance or redemption workflow. This recognises recorded engagement, not improved batting, and no phone use at training is required.

Can players ask for help or share a video?

Yes. Players can request a Coach Conversation and choose a preferred club member, or leave it to the coaching team. Coaches can attach a YouTube link to a Match or Training Observation; players can include one in their request or My Innings entry. Video files are uploaded to YouTube. Club Batting stores only the link and optional note; discussion and next steps stay in the app. To show one ball, share a separately trimmed video, or use a start-time link with a note identifying the few seconds to watch. A start-time link does not hide the rest of the video. An unlisted video can be viewed and forwarded by anyone with its link.
