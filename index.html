A few useful answers

Before you start.

Can we use Teams & availability on its own?

Yes. Import your playing list and add players when needed. A batting subscription or Player Plan is not required.

Does everyone need an account before we can select teams?

No. Selectors can add and select players straight away. Players need a linked account to manage their own availability and receive team messages in the app.

Can we explore before signing up?

Yes. Both demos use fictional players, need no account and send no messages. Try batting development or team selection.

How does our club get started?

Start a free trial: 60 days for Club Batting and 21 days for Teams & availability. Try either product or both; each keeps its own trial length. Choose when your trial starts and confirm your email. No card or approval is required.
