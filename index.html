A few practical things

Before you jump in.

Can I explore without an account?

Yes. The demo opens a fictional club with every stage completed. Use its Explore menu to move between the Workshop, How We Bat, Player Plans, training and feedback. Example edits last until you reset or reload.

Does every club use the same batting philosophy?

Your club defines its approach. The Philosophy Lead and nominated coaches shape How We Bat, then players develop individual plans using prompts based on that approach. The Philosophy Lead can edit those prompts before confirming them.

Who gets the club started?

A Club Admin looks after setup and access. The Philosophy Lead brings the club’s batting approach together with nominated coaches. Players can then build their plans, practise and add feedback with their coaches.

How do we try it with our own club?

Use Try it with your club to email David. Share your club’s name and what you’d like to work on so we can arrange the next step. Existing players and coaches can sign in here.
