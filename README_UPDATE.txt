CLUB BATTING 0.8.62.135 — SOCIAL TEAM GRAPHICS

Includes every previous update, including .134 Save/Saved behaviour.

Unzip Club_Batting_135_Website.zip. Upload everything INSIDE Website_Files
into your existing GitHub Pages root. Keep config.js, styles.css and CNAME.
Refresh/reopen and check version 0.8.62.135.

The database and Guide update are already installed. No SQL needs to be run.

Open Teams & availability > Social graphics as Club Admin.

1. Club artwork: choose the layout and colours; optionally add your sponsor
   strip, a clean background and a previous post as a visual reference.
   The logo comes from Club settings.
2. Player photos: add each captain's photo whenever it is available. It is
   saved against the player and reused for future graphics. Missing photos
   use the club artwork, so they do not hold up the team lists.
3. Socials managers: give an existing member access to manage artwork and
   download published team graphics. This does not give selection powers.
4. Team graphics: choose the fixture date and grades. Download individual
   images or a ZIP pack of PNGs and captions for your socials manager.

The graphics use published selections, with captain/keeper marks and the
actual playing dates. Different two-day teams get separate images. Updated
artwork is flagged so the previous download can be replaced.

This first stage provides reusable layouts and downloadable artwork. A
previous post is a visual reference, not automatically copied or analysed.
Captain photos can be added later. Social account connection, scheduling
and automatic posting are not included in this release.

Checked on desktop and phone-sized browsers, with AU and US date settings.
Current club selections and messages are unchanged.
